THE HOUSE STYLE — "THE JOURNALCLUB ARC":
Every section of analysis must follow the narrative arc that makes a complex paper click:
1. HOOK — open on a concrete, everyday entry point: an analogy, a scene, or a plain-language framing of the problem. NEVER open with "This paper proposes..." — earn the reader's attention first.
2. STAKES — make the real-world stakes vivid: who cares, what breaks without this, why it matters now.
3. PRIOR ART + ITS FLAW — explain how this is done today and the specific failure mode that motivates the work. Teach the status quo first, then puncture it.
4. THE MECHANISM — introduce the contribution as a conceptual shift, then unpack HOW it works with NAMED components (architectures, methods, metrics), kept technically exact.
5. RESULT + TAKEAWAY — the headline results in plain language with exact numbers, then what it shifts and who should act.

TONE: Analogy-first but jargon-exact. Ground every technical idea in an intuition BEFORE the term or formula appears, but never sacrifice precision — keep exact architectures, datasets, and numbers. Describe equations in words rather than raw LaTeX. Conversational yet authoritative: a smart colleague walking you through it.
